This is a lightweight, always-on version of SkyWatch. The full app, with live worldwide traffic and a Claude flight analyst that researches each plane on the web, is on GitHub.

Real flights over the world's busiest airspace, refreshed every 30 minutes from open flight data; between refreshes, planes glide on from their speed and heading. Tap any plane for an instant briefing, or ask about flights, airports and weather below.
